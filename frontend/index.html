    Where the data comes from

    CPCB real-time AQI feed, published on data.gov.in. Limits are the Indian NAAQS (2009). Run scripts/fetch_datagov.py hourly with a free API key to collect snapshots automatically.

    Scores come from one hourly reading per station compared with 24-hour legal limits. Treat them as a snapshot, not a health verdict.
